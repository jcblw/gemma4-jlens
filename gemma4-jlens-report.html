Gemma 4 under the Jacobian Lens: four experiments

Anthropic's Jacobian Lens reads out what a transformer's internal activations are "poised to verbalize" — transporting each layer's residual stream into the final-layer basis and decoding it with the model's own unembedding. These four probes show four different shapes of computation: an explicit entity waypoint, a concept-only retrieval, a late faint leak, and negation held as an emoji-laden falsehood concept.

Does the company name leak? A business question through the lens

The prompt describes a company without naming it. In the boot-shaped-country experiment, the hidden entity (Italy) surfaced explicitly in the middle layers. Here we test whether the same happens with a company.

Prompt

Model's actual next-token top 8

Result: the company name never appears. Across all 41 layers, no company token enters the top 8 at this position. Where the geography prompt routed through an explicit entity (“Italy”), this one routes through the abstract concept — monetization → revenue models → advertising — suggesting the fact is stored against the business concept, not the entity. Negation through the lens: the model represents “false” in emoji

The prompt ends in “is not”, forcing the model to hold a negation while it works out what to say. The lens reveals how it stores that: as an explicit falsehood concept — ❌ 🚫 🙅 untrue falsehood misconception — for ten layers before any city appears.

Prompt

Fact: The capital of France is not ▁

Model's actual next-token top 8
Paris London Berlin Rome Lyon always in the

Top prediction is “Paris” — it's starting a sentence like “…is not Paris, but rather…” or correcting a false premise.

Finding: negation is a first-class concept in the middle layers. Layers 8–19 hold ❌, 🚫, 🙅, a winking 😉, and words like untrue · falsehood · misconception · wrong — the model marks “a false statement is in play” long before it retrieves any geography. At L21 the readout even turns contrastive in three languages (बल्कि · 而是 · 也不是 — “but rather”). Paris doesn't enter the top 8 until L30.
